Booking & payments

  • You can cancel a booking before it's accepted at no charge.

Account

  • You can delete your account at any time from the Profile tab in the app — this permanently removes your account and associated data.
  • Trouble signing in? Email us and we'll help you sort it out.

Reporting an issue

  • Disputes about a completed session can be filed directly from the order screen.
  • Reviews that seem inappropriate can be reported directly where they're shown in the app.
  • For anything else, email us and we'll respond as soon as we can.
